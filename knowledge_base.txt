ABOUT BGL

BGL is an industrial technology company based in Delhi NCR, India, specializing in energy efficiency and clean-air solutions for commercial and industrial clients. Our client base includes manufacturing groups, hospitality chains, and commercial real estate operators, including names like JBM Group, Hilton, and Hyatt.

PRODUCT LINE: ENERGY OPTIMIZATION SYSTEM (ES-25)

Our flagship product, BGL ES-25, helps industrial and commercial facilities reduce energy consumption by 15-30% through smart monitoring, predictive maintenance recommendations, and automated load balancing. The system integrates with existing HVAC and electrical infrastructure without requiring a full hardware overhaul.

INSTALLATION PROCESS

A typical ES-25 installation takes 2-4 weeks depending on facility size. The process includes: (1) an initial energy audit conducted on-site by a BGL engineer, (2) sensor and controller installation, (3) a two-week calibration period where the system learns the facility's usage patterns, and (4) a handover session with staff training on the monitoring dashboard.

WARRANTY AND SUPPORT

All BGL systems come with a 3-year warranty covering hardware defects and firmware updates. Support is available via a dedicated account manager, with a target response time of 4 business hours for critical issues (system down, safety-related) and 24 hours for standard requests (dashboard questions, minor calibration tweaks).

PRICING MODEL

BGL uses an outcome-linked pricing model: clients pay a lower upfront installation fee plus a percentage of verified energy savings over the first 24 months. This aligns BGL's incentives with actual client outcomes rather than just equipment sales. Typical upfront fees range from 40-60% of a comparable fixed-price competitor installation, with the remainder recovered through the savings-share arrangement.

CLIENT ONBOARDING

New clients go through a structured onboarding process: a kickoff call to align on goals and timeline, a site assessment by a BGL engineer, a customized rollout plan specific to the facility, and a 90-day review meeting to measure actual savings against the initial baseline audit.

COMPATIBILITY WITH EXISTING INFRASTRUCTURE

The ES-25 system is designed to retrofit onto existing HVAC and electrical infrastructure in most cases. Systems installed after 2005 are generally compatible without modification. Facilities with pre-2005 HVAC systems may require an adapter kit, which BGL provides at an additional cost determined during the site audit.

WHAT HAPPENS IF SAVINGS TARGETS AREN'T MET

Because pricing is partially outcome-linked, BGL has a direct incentive to hit savings targets. If actual savings fall short of the agreed baseline at the 90-day review, the account team revisits calibration settings at no extra cost to the client, and the savings-share percentage is adjusted downward for that period.

MULTI-SITE AND PORTFOLIO CLIENTS

For clients with multiple facilities (e.g., hotel chains, manufacturing groups with several plants), BGL offers a portfolio rollout program: a pilot installation at one site, a 90-day performance review, and then a phased rollout across remaining sites with pricing terms locked in from the pilot.

CANCELLATION AND CONTRACT TERMS

Clients may cancel the outcome-linked pricing agreement after the initial 24-month period with 60 days' written notice. Early cancellation within the first 24 months requires payment of the remaining upfront-fee balance, prorated based on installation date.

CONTACT FOR ESCALATIONS

Standard support requests go through the assigned account manager. For urgent escalations outside business hours, clients can reach BGL's support line directly (see your onboarding packet for the current number) — this assistant does not have real-time visibility into open support tickets.
